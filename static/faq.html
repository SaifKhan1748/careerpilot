Frequently Asked Questions

Is this free?

Yes - this is a student-built tool using free-tier APIs.

Will the AI ever make up things I haven't done?

No - every resume claim is checked against your actual submitted evidence (skills, projects, experience). Anything unsupported is flagged, not silently included.

Can other people see my resume or data?

No - every account's data is private to that account only.

Why did my score not reach 90+?

The score reflects how well your real, submitted evidence matches the job's requirements. A low score usually means a genuine skills gap, not a bug - check the Skill Gaps tab for what to work on.

Does this guarantee I'll get hired?

No - it helps you prepare and tailor materials, but hiring outcomes depend on many factors outside this tool's control.

Something's not working - what do I do?

Contact the site owner with what you were doing and what error you saw.
