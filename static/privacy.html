Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your name, email, phone number, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text). When you use the app, we store your resume text, job descriptions you submit, and the AI-generated results (matches, scores, interview answers) tied to your account.

How we use it

Your data is used only to power your own account's features - resume tailoring, matching, interview practice, and application tracking. Resume and job text is sent to Groq's API for AI processing under their standard API terms.

Who can see your data

Only you. Every account's data is isolated - other users cannot see your resume, applications, or results.

Data retention

Your data stays until you request deletion. Contact the site owner to request account and data removal.

Third parties

We use Groq (AI processing) and DuckDuckGo (company research search). No data is sold or shared for advertising.
